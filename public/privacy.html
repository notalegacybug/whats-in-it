Privacy policy

Ingredient Safety (whats-in-it.pages.dev and the Android app). Last updated 16 November 2026.

In short

The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data. Everything you scan or save stays on your phone.

Photos and the camera

When you take or choose a photo of a label, the text is read on your phone. The photo is not uploaded or stored by us. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.

What is saved, and where

  • Products you save, your scan history, and any research notes you add are stored on your phone only.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is stored on your phone.
  • You can delete a saved product from My scans. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes everything.

What the app downloads

The app downloads its ingredient reference and the text-reading engine from this website. These downloads contain no information about you or your products.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not use that data to identify you.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or contact details.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Not medical advice

Results show whether an ingredient appears on published hazard and allergen lists. They are not medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: open an issue on the project page.
